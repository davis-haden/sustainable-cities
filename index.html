Chapter 1 · The problem

Where we live shapes every breath

More than half of the world's people live in cities, and that share keeps growing. Cities are where most of our energy gets used, most of our trips get taken and most of our emissions get made.

Chapter 2 · The plan

Three moves to a green city

The UN's Sustainable Development Goal 11 asks for cities that are inclusive, safe, resilient and sustainable. The model Atlanta at the top of this page gets there in three moves, and real cities are making each one today.

  1. 01

    Clean power

    2026–2043

    Coal stacks and fuel tanks give way to wind turbines, solar fields and a low-carbon reactor. Cleaner power means cleaner air for every neighborhood downwind.

  2. 02

    Light rail

    2043–2059

    Transportation makes up nearly a third of greenhouse gas emissions. Two rail lines replace car-choked roads, and electric vehicles take over the trips that are left.

  3. 03

    Nature

    2059–2076

    Trees and open ground cool hot streets and soak up storm water. Atlanta's BeltLine shows how a city can track what new canopy and less pavement do for its climate resilience.
