[ 01 — WHY IT MATTERS ]

Where we live shapes every breath

Dirty air doesn’t stay in our lungs. It reaches hospitals, airports, schools and paychecks, until a whole city feels it. Why It Matters follows two places where the air turned dangerous: a capital city choking on smog, and islands buried in smoke that drifted across borders.

What did it cost them, and what did they do about it?
