    Three phases to a green city

    1. 01

      Clean power

      2000–2017

      Coal stacks, factories and tank farms give way to wind turbines, solar fields and a low-carbon reactor.

    2. 02

      Light rail

      2017–2033

      Two light-rail lines grow out from a central interchange, replacing car-choked roads and gas stations.

    3. 03

      Nature

      2033–2050

      The river is restored, clear-cut land grows back as forest, and vacant lots become community gardens.
